Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Local deliveries typically arrive within 2–5 business days. Express options are available at checkout.

How do I return an item?

Open a return request in your account, print the return label, and drop the package at the nearest partner courier.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Visa/MasterCard, PayPal, Pesapal, and Stripe for international cards.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — international shipping is available. Rates and delivery times vary by destination.
